# Bright Mindful Pages

## Brand identity package

Bright Mindful Pages creates structured, gentle mental-health companions for daily self-improvement and tracking.

### Brand mission

Provide simple, organized tools that help adults notice how they are doing, build supportive habits, and reflect without pressure.

### Key themes

- Mindfulness
- Habit tracking
- Structured self-care
- Zero-pressure reflection
- Mood and anxiety awareness
- Physical wellness indicators

### Target audience

Adults looking for approachable, organized ways to map anxiety levels, moods, daily habits, and physical wellness indicators.

## Approved logo direction

### Gentle Companion

The approved identity uses a page-and-seed emblem:

- The page represents structure, reflection, and a safe place to return to.
- The seed represents gradual growth, gentle progress, and becoming.
- The small glow/sun detail adds optimism without creating a loud or overly cheerful tone.

The identity should feel warm, adult-friendly, organized, and emotionally safe. It should never feel clinical, childish, mystical, or pressuring.

## Logo system

### Primary stacked lockup

Use when introducing the brand, on the front of a KDP cover, or anywhere the full identity needs to be established.

### Horizontal lockup

Use for narrow headers, author pages, website navigation, and back covers.

### Compact emblem

Use for social avatars, book spines, page corners, stamps, and small thumbnail placements. The emblem should remain recognizable without lettering.

### One-color version

Use for embossing, foil, rubber stamps, interior pages, monochrome printing, and small-format reproduction. Keep the page outline, seed, and glow as one solid ink treatment.

## Typography

- **Lora** — primary display and brand wordmark direction; warm, literary, and grown-up.
- **DM Sans** — supporting copy, labels, usage notes, and practical information; clear and approachable.

## Brand voice

Use language that is:

- Gentle, clear, and encouraging
- Practical rather than clinical
- Observant rather than judgmental
- Structured without sounding rigid
- Hopeful without making promises about health outcomes

Preferred phrases:

- “A little room to grow.”
- “Gentle progress without pressure.”
- “A small daily companion.”
- “A gentle place to notice, organize, and return.”

Avoid:

- Medical or therapeutic claims
- Language that implies guaranteed transformation
- Shame-based habit language
- Overly cute or childish wording
- Pressure, urgency, or perfection language

## KDP and social guidance

- Keep the emblem clear at small sizes and in thumbnails.
- Use the stacked lockup on front covers when space allows.
- Use the horizontal lockup on back covers and narrow headers.
- Reserve the emblem alone for avatars and small marks.
- Maintain generous clear space around the logo.
- Keep the logo legible against quiet, warm backgrounds.
- Use the one-color version whenever printing or reproduction could reduce detail.
